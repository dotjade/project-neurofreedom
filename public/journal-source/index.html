A SMALL PAUSE IN YOUR DAY

You don’t have to
earn a gentler moment.

A gentle idea for today. Try one if it suits you, choose another, or rest. There is nothing to finish or catch up on. You do not need to hold back tics.

These ideas are not proven tic or ADHD treatments. Stop if anything is uncomfortable.

Gentle movement

A hand at ease

Gently open and rest one hand in your usual supported position, if that feels comfortable.

Choose your version

Seated: Use your usual supported seated position. No stretching or stillness is required.

Standing: If standing is already comfortable, keep your usual support and let the hand rest naturally.

Rest: Imagine the movement, or rest. There is nothing to complete.

Your usual supports

A familiar little route

Consider a short stroll or wheel along a familiar level route, using your usual supports.

Choose your version

Seated: Stay seated and notice something familiar around you instead.

Standing: Choose only a route and movement that already feel comfortable for you.

Rest: Stay where you are, imagine the route, or skip it.

A quiet moment

Notice one comfortable thing

Notice one comfortable sound, shape, or texture nearby.

Choose your version

Seated: Look or listen from wherever you are comfortably seated.

Standing: If you are already comfortable standing, notice something nearby.

Rest: No need to pay close attention. Rest or skip freely.

Ordinary leisure

Familiar music, or silence

Choose familiar quiet music if you would enjoy it. Silence is an equal choice.

Choose your version

Seated: Listen in your usual comfortable position if you choose.

Standing: There is no need to move, dance, or keep a rhythm.

Rest: Choose silence, rest, or leave this idea for another day.

A little play

A small mark or a silly sentence

Make a small mark, or say, type, or imagine a silly sentence.

Choose your version

Seated: Use a familiar comfortable way to draw, speak, or type.

Standing: If you prefer standing, keep your usual support. Imagining works too.

Rest: Do nothing, or imagine something amusing. Either is enough.

An equal choice

Rest, or leave the page

Rest can be the whole idea. There is no progress to keep up with.

Choose your version

Seated: Settle into your usual comfortable seated position if you want.

Standing: Change to a usual comfortable position if that suits you.

Rest: Close the page or simply carry on with your day.
