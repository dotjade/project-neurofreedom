Completed a synthetic cycle-detection benchmark: 240 matched replicate trajectories per scenario, seven scenarios and three history lengths produced 5,040 evaluations. Eleven implementation checks pass. An independent numerical audit reproduced selected forecasts, all summary/bootstrap metrics, source-hash provenance and nine zero/constant/linear edge cases. This is software/methods validation under selected simulation assumptions, not a clinical study, medical-device validation or biological finding. The source, tests, summarized results, metadata and logs are archived in analyses/cycle_detection; full replicate rows and figures can be regenerated using the documented command.

Key methods lesson: a good repeating fit can be misleading; compare forecasts with appropriate nonperiodic baselines, test unseen observations and obtain independent measurements before attributing an observed pattern to biology. A successful prediction alone cannot identify its mechanism.
