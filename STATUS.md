Eleven implementation checks pass. An independent numerical audit reproduced selected forecasts, all summary/bootstrap metrics, source-hash provenance and nine zero/constant/linear edge cases. Key methods lesson: a good repeating fit can be misleading; compare forecasts with appropriate nonperiodic baselines, test unseen observations and obtain independent measurements before attributing an observed pattern to biology. A successful prediction alone cannot identify its mechanism.
