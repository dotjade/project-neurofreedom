# From a single leak to testable causal alternatives

28 October 2026 | Requested mechanistic synthesis, not a new daily scan | Hypotheses and synthetic methods, not a biological discovery

## The useful pattern

There may be a controllable bottleneck, but current evidence does not establish one universal chemical leak or brain location that initiates every tic disorder. The more defensible working account is **heterogeneous susceptibility interacting with momentary input and action-control state**. This is a research priority, not proof that distributed models are correct or that a simple effective intervention is impossible.

Three questions must be separated: why susceptibility develops and persists; why a particular tic occurs now; and why symptoms cause continuing disability. A signal preceding movement can reflect preparation or compensation. A treatment-sensitive node can interrupt expression without being the original fault. Conversely, several causes can converge on a useful single control point. Neither complexity nor a genetic contribution establishes irreversibility.

The narrow candidate worth testing is **whether input-specific variability interacts with the time needed to reconfigure control to predict subsequent tic events, beyond either measure alone**. Existing researchers already study neural noise, state transitions, predictive retuning and perception-action binding. Our contribution is the explicit comparison, counterevidence and decision criteria below, not ownership of those ideas or a claim of an overlooked cure.

## Ranked research hypotheses

Ranks mean priority for discriminating research, not probability of truth, treatment ranking or clinical advice. These accounts can coexist, so model comparison must allow combinations without unlimited post-hoc flexibility.

### 1. State-dependent input-to-action transfer, not uniformly weak brakes

**Prediction:** an independently measured input-variability by retuning-lag interaction predicts future tic probability beyond additive variability, lag, recent tics, ordinary movement and arousal. The timing of vulnerability should be reproducible across days and prespecified contexts, not selected after seeing the outcome. Later task performance may be relatively preserved.

**Why prioritize it:** the intact-versus-impaired automatic-inhibition disagreement and rapid compensation after a task switch challenge a universal constant-inhibition account. Animal input-dependence provides a causal example of interacting vulnerability and trigger, without proving the human mechanism.

**Counterevidence:** Kurvits' symptom correlations were null; EEG variability, aperiodic activity and behavioral variability are not interchangeable quantities. The adult predictive-retuning study's fitted parameters did not differ between groups, and its authors could not distinguish predictive coding from binding. These findings lower confidence in tonic neural noise or a universal learning-parameter deficit.

**Falsifier:** the interaction repeatedly adds no held-out tic prediction despite reliable measurements and sufficient precision, predicts only ordinary movement errors, or disappears with matched attention/artifact controls. A state-independent single generator with better prospective performance and valid selective engagement would favor a simpler account. Do not salvage the hypothesis by redefining the relevant state after a null result.

**Feasibility and exact prerequisite:** no verified openly licensed, nonidentifying human aggregate dataset here contains aligned event timing, input measures and independently estimated compensation lag with suitable controls. The human test is therefore not performed. An adequately governed dataset or published sufficient aggregate interaction estimates would be required; obtaining participant data or recruiting people is not part of this work. First specify an operational assay mapping and a fixed model comparison before any data request or clinical design.

### 2. Different developmental routes converge on a modifiable functional state

**Prediction:** distinct etiologic models share some functional disturbance even when cell counts or anatomy differ. Restoring a mature functional state and preventing an early developmental change are different tests; success in one does not imply success in the other.

**Why prioritize it:** genetic heterogeneity and circuit convergence can be reconciled without demanding one initiating mutation or lesion. HDC-knockout evidence with normal interneuron numbers directly challenges obligatory structural loss as the common explanation, while leaving functional involvement open.

**Counterevidence:** small severe-adult postmortem samples cannot establish whether cell differences caused symptoms, resulted from long illness, or reflect selection and other influences. Organoids model development, not demonstrated adult repair. Some genetic models lack predicted behavioral phenotypes, and cross-network enrichment is not robust across databases.

**Falsifier:** candidate functional signatures fail to replicate across etiologic models, or apparently shared effects disappear after assay, developmental-stage and general motor controls. A signature confined to one genotype would support an endotype, not a universal cause.

**Feasible next step:** complete the gene-level chronic-tic versus OCD/combined evidence table only from a safely separated public aggregate gene table. This run audits union counts and negative evidence but does not claim to have completed all 36 gene-level classifications. Mixed individual variant/pedigree tables were not ingested. No human treatment target follows directly from gene association.

### 3. Event probability and event identity are partly separable

**Prediction:** an independently estimated binding/unbinding parameter predicts which recurrent action pattern occurs beyond overall tic rate, while a permissive-state parameter better predicts event probability. The combined account must beat single-factor models on held-out observations, not merely fit the same data more flexibly.

**Why prioritize it:** excitability alone does not specify organized recurrent action content. Adult perception-action binding findings and animal temporal-versus-spatial dissociation motivate this comparison. It does not make tics voluntary or reduce TS to an acquired habit.

**Counterevidence:** laboratory binding associations are not demonstrated natural-tic mechanisms; another task-switching study found altered EEG but unchanged behavior. Comorbid symptoms and general learning can explain apparent differences. Conscious urges cannot be required before every tic, including those documented during sleep.

**Falsifier:** rate and pattern predictions do not dissociate, or binding signatures reflect generic task performance without independent tic-pattern information. The current public aggregate reports cannot evaluate this test. A standardized, permitted nonidentifying aggregate transition representation and externally estimated predictors are missing prerequisites.

## What we actually calculated

### A causal-identification counterexample, explicitly synthetic

The [model report](model/report.md), inputs, code and results enumerate three artificial architectures: one modeled upstream input, parallel inputs and coincident inputs, each with a permissive gate. All were deliberately calibrated to event probability .10 in one context and .16 in another. Halving the downstream gate produces .05 and .08 in every architecture. Yet ideal input-specific blocks distinguish some architectures, and complete abolition under one block still cannot distinguish a single input from one necessary member of a coincidence mechanism.

**Actual result:** these particular baseline/context/downstream contrasts cannot identify a unique upstream cause. This is not full observational equivalence: input marginals differ and sufficiently valid measurements could distinguish them. The gate remains another necessary component even in the one-upstream-input construction. No parameter was fitted to a person, no seed was required for exact enumeration, and no biological mechanism or treatment effect was discovered. Ten implementation tests and 113 independent closed-form checks passed.

A separate fixed-marginal construction changes joint input probability without changing either marginal, showing why averages alone cannot identify a coincidence mechanism. It does not show that human tics arise from neural synchrony. A hypothetical ordinary-action channel makes the need to measure preserved function explicit, not a claim that real treatments necessarily suppress general movement.

### Two bounded public-aggregate audits

The [genetic arithmetic audit](genetics/report.md) verifies that 36 is not the count of chronic-tic-specific high-confidence genes and that the combined sample includes previously reported participants. Database-specific convergence and negative transmitted/synonymous comparisons are retained rather than discarded.

The [trial interval audit](perturbations/report.md) finds that both reported pediatric deutetrabenazine 95% confidence intervals exclude an average benefit of at least five YGTSS total-tic points under the tested conditions, while both allow three points; four points is inside only one interval. These are explicitly illustrative thresholds chosen after viewing estimates, not clinical meaningfulness cutoffs, equivalence tests, a pooled result or evidence against all dopamine involvement. Target engagement and adult extrapolation remain unresolved.

## Inclusive scope and meaningful outcomes

Persistent severe adult symptoms are not a failure to follow an assumed remission trajectory. Evidence from children, selected responders, surgical cohorts, mild volunteers and adults cannot be substituted without checking eligibility and estimands. Waxing and waning, sleep changes and developmental improvement are possible modifiers, not universal explanations.

A mechanism useful for tic frequency may not explain pain, injury, fatigue, social participation, communication, OCD, ADHD, anxiety or broader daily function. These outcomes and medication/comorbidity effects require separate measurement; changes in one do not prove changes in all. A marker could be a cause, modulator, compensatory response or consequence. The study design must discriminate these roles rather than infer them from a correlation.

## Progress, uncertainty and next decision

Progress is measured by evidence quality, explicit rival predictions, falsification and reproducibility, followed by independent biological validation and ultimately meaningful durable clinical benefit. This run advances synthesis and reproducible inference checks. It does **not** advance to a validated new mechanism, intervention or cure. Paper counts, time spent and a subjective score cannot substitute for those milestones.

The next bounded decision is to finish the safely accessible gene-specific evidence map and write a fixed operational comparison for hypothesis 1, retaining the alternative that no tic-specific timing interaction exists. Do not initiate human experiments or repeat completed audits. If appropriate aggregate inputs remain unavailable, record that prerequisite rather than inventing a fitted result.

Coverage is purposive and English-language-heavy, not a systematic review or exhaustive novelty search. Modules record exact queries, primary identifiers, official registry checks, inaccessible material and ambiguous source reporting. Peer-reviewed human associations, clinical trials, preclinical experiments, registry metadata and our hypotheses/synthetic calculations remain distinct. Registry entries are not outcome evidence. No anecdote or preprint is treated as established causation. No individual/family medical data, identifiable community material, unlicensed full text, intervention, contact or recruitment was stored or performed.
