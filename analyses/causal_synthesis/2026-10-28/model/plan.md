# Pre-computation specification: causal control is not causal origin

Written 28 October 2026 before execution. This is an analyst-specified **synthetic mathematical demonstration**, not a clinical preregistration, fitted model, new biological finding or simulation of a particular person. It is separate from the earlier cycle-forecast and paired-pain exercises.

## Prediction

Different upstream mechanisms can produce the same observed event rate, contextual modulation and response to a downstream suppressive control. Even eliminating events by selectively disabling one node need not identify a unique source, because a node can be a necessary component of a multi-input mechanism.

## Fixed construction

Use a binary illustrative event, two candidate binary inputs X and Z, and a permissive gate G. Three artificial worlds are:

- Single: event = X AND G, P(X)=0.2; Z is irrelevant.
- Parallel: event = (X OR Z) AND G, independent equal input probabilities 1-sqrt(0.8).
- Coincidence: event = (X AND Z) AND G, independent equal probabilities sqrt(0.2).

All have pre-gate event probability 0.2 by construction. The two arbitrary gate contexts have probabilities 0.5 and 0.8; these are not identified stress states, dopamine measurements or clinical risk strata. Downstream suppression halves gate probability. Selective ideal interventions set X, Z or both to zero. Independent ordinary-action demand has probability 0.4 and also passes through this hypothetical shared gate. This particular implementation will suppress ordinary actions; it does not assume that every real intervention does so.

Compute exact finite-state expectations by enumeration, cross-check with closed forms, and retain floating-point error tolerance. No random draws or seed. Primary outputs: full scenario event rates; ordinary-action rates; equivalence across worlds before and during downstream control; differences under ideal input interventions. No uncertainty intervals or causal probabilities about people will be calculated.

Separately, hold both input marginal probabilities at 0.25 and vary their joint probability across its Fréchet range, 0 to 0.25. This tests what marginal activity alone cannot identify about AND-type coincidence. It is not an observed neuronal synchrony result.

## What this can change

The analysis may rule out an inference rule, not a biological mechanism: equal symptom responses or event abolition do not logically establish a unique originating lesion. In real research, independent target engagement, appropriate timing, specificity for unwanted versus ordinary action and alternative-node perturbations are prerequisites. No human perturbation, medication change or data collection is proposed for execution here.

## Limits and falsification

The alternatives were deliberately calibrated to agree, so matching is not empirical evidence for any world. The construction omits recurrent dynamics, adaptation, urges, learning, comorbidity, functional burden and harm. Perfect selectivity is hypothetical. Failure of the stated identities is a programming/derivation error, not disproof of a Tourette hypothesis. Human adjudication requires permitted independently observed data and valid assays that are not supplied by this computation.
