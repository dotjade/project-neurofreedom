# Bounded prediction and stopping rule

Recorded 22 October 2026, 02:57 UTC, after repository review and initial orientation to the primary article, but before detailed aggregate extraction. This is a working prediction, not a prospective preregistration.

The 2024 Okun report will not identify a single interchangeable clinical/function estimand: a blinded randomized-order within-visit video contrast, preoperative-to-six-month recalled severity, and MOS-36 longitudinal health/function scores have different exposure windows and comparators. A public aggregate comparison specifying the same treatment regimes, follow-up clock, outcome recall window, eligible denominator and uncertainty would weaken that prediction and permit a narrower compatible contrast.

Inspect primary methods and aggregate tables, official trial metadata and protocol/SAP where lawful. Preserve null outcomes and documentation inconsistencies. No individual rows may be retained, reconstructed or analyzed; no missing covariance, scores, or denominators may be invented. Stop without an efficacy calculation if a compatible functional contrast is not established. Establish the exact missing public aggregate prerequisite and a distinct next decision rather than repeat the prior resource-feasibility audit.
