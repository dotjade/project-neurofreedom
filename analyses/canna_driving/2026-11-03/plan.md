# Prospective bounded driving audit

Written 2026-11-03 before full-text extraction and computation. Historical CANNA-TICS same-trial substudy, not independent replication or individual driving advice.

Question: Does the driving substudy establish confirmatory noninferiority or broadly safe driving, given the primary superiority gate, chosen margin, analysis population, missingness and endpoint?

Prediction: a selected short-term computerized endpoint and a passed numerical margin cannot by themselves establish confirmatory noninferiority after a failed upstream superiority gate, or real-world road safety. Disconfirming evidence would show a valid independent confirmatory hierarchy, justified clinically acceptable margin, consistent intention-to-treat/per-protocol analyses, robust missingness handling and direct real-world safety ascertainment. Preserve actual favorable results and do not infer harm or no benefit from uncertainty.

Method: verify primary driving paper, protocol, main trial and registry identities/dates; extract aggregate counts, contrasts, intervals, test hierarchy, imputation, population and harms. If counts permit, compute deterministic extreme-case missingness bounds for the randomized substudy binary endpoint, clearly distinguishing descriptive identification bounds from confidence intervals and adjusted model estimates. No participant files, clinical action, author contact or paid compute. No random number generation or seed planned. Missing quantities remain explicit prerequisites.
