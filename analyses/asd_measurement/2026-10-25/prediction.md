# Prediction before aggregate computation

Date: 25 October 2026. This task follows the October 24 STATUS decision, not a new discovery scan.

Prediction: adjustment for enrollment age in a pooled cross-sectional model will not identify an adult-specific ASD impairment contrast or establish that communication differences cause lower measured impairment. Counterevidence would be explicit adult-stratified model estimates with denominators and uncertainty, an age-by-ASD interaction with sufficient support, or independent measurement/informant validation that tests the proposed explanation.

Method: inspect official primary Methods, Tables 1–3, Discussion, limitations and supplement inventory. Map age coverage and estimands. Compute only published-aggregate sample coverage, feasible model-overlap bounds and simple ASD-minus-non-ASD contrasts. Preserve unadjusted and adjusted nulls. Do not infer age distribution from a mean, rebuild participants, estimate missing model composition or decompose adjustment into causal effects. No random input or simulation is used.

Stopping rule: absent adult-stratified and independently validated burden measures, close this paper-specific audit and name the prerequisite rather than request participant data or repeat the analysis.
