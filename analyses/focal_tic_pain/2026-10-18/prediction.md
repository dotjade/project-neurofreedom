# Pre-extraction question and decision rule

Recorded 2026-10-18 03:57 UTC, after reading current repository protocol/status and the October 18 pain-interference audit, before retrieving the Marras primary article.

## Bounded question
Does Marras et al. (2001), DOI 10.1212/WNL.56.5.605, PMID 11245710, supply a separately defined tic-related pain-benefit or pain-interference outcome from a randomized controlled focal-tic intervention, distinguishable from tic frequency/severity, premonitory urge/sensation and injection/procedure discomfort?

## Prediction
The abstract-level endpoint list and the prior audit suggest that a compatible pain-benefit outcome will not be identifiable. A complete lawful primary methods-and-tables inspection is required to confirm that prediction. Abstract silence alone cannot establish endpoint absence.

## Disconfirming finding
A full primary methods/table/supplement specifies a pain-intensity or pain-interference benefit outcome, with its instrument/question, timing and treatment-comparison data separately from tic endpoints and treatment adverse effects. Such a finding would weaken or disconfirm the predicted measurement gap. It need not show a beneficial effect.

## Extraction and analytical gate
Verify the primary source, eligibility/ages and severity, enrollment and analyzed denominators, crossover sequence and assessment/washout timing, carryover handling, endpoint definitions, comparative estimates/uncertainty, negative findings, and relevant adverse effects. A pain-effect computation requires a compatible pain endpoint, verified outcome-specific denominators, a contrast and enough uncertainty information for the crossover design. Do not borrow tic improvement, global change, premonitory sensations or procedural discomfort as a pain-benefit measure. Do not treat repeated treatment periods as independent groups.

If primary full methods/tables cannot be lawfully inspected, stop with an access-limited conclusion and exact prerequisites. If full methods show no compatible endpoint, close this one-trial question without pain-effect computation. A narrow arithmetic check is allowed only if it answers the bounded question and its inputs are verified; absence of a defensible check is an acceptable result. No participant-level rows, quotes, paper copies, contact, payment, clinical action or GitHub writes. Suggest but do not execute a distinct next question.
