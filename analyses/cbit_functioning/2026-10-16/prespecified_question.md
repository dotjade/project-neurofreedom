# Prospective bounded question

Recorded 16 October 2026, 02:40 UTC, after reading the repository README, protocol, status and 15 October maintenance report, but before retrieving the McGuire functioning report.

## Prediction

In the same randomized CBIT versus psychoeducation/supportive therapy trial (ages 16–69), broader functional improvement will be less secure than the established acute tic-severity result: acute randomized disability/social/family contrasts will be endpoint-specific, and positive responder associations or selected follow-up will not by themselves establish a general randomized functional benefit, a tic-mediated causal pathway, or off-treatment durability.

## Result that would weaken or disconfirm this prediction

Consistent prespecified randomized acute improvements across direct disability, social and family functioning outcomes, with clear instrument direction, adequate uncertainty, multiplicity control and follow-up of the randomized sample preserving the comparison, would weaken this restriction. An adequately powered prespecified equivalence result could support a narrower absence-of-important-effect claim; simple nonsignificance cannot.
