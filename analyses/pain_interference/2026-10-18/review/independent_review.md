# Independent source-method review

Reviewed 18 October 2026. Scope: Taylor, Anderson and Davies (2022), DOI 10.3389/fpsyt.2022.914044; public questionnaire availability, measurement interpretation, age scope, and publication status. This is not a second arithmetic audit. No participant narratives or individual-level data were used in this review.

## Verdict

The article supports a descriptive, mixed-age comparison of reported domain means in a self-selected, pain-experiencing sample. It does not provide the information needed to reconstruct adult-only estimates or paired inference.

## What the source establishes

- Recruitment required current tics and tic-related pain, suspected or confirmed tic disorder, English literacy, and age at least 16. Recruitment was online through patient organizations and support channels. This is not a sample from which pain prevalence among everyone with tic disorders can be estimated.
- The sample spans ages 16–71. The youngest published band combines ages 16–18. Calling participants adults does not make the results an age-18-and-over analysis.
- The adapted interference measure has 13 domains, a previous-week recall period, scores from 1 to 5, and an N/A choice. Table 3 reports domain means and SDs for tics and tic-related pain. It reports no domain-specific sample sizes.
- The exact endpoint wording published in the methods is 1: “No interference at all upon my daily life”; 5: “Severe interference at all upon my daily life”. The latter wording is reproduced as published, not silently corrected. Only endpoints, rather than all response labels or complete question stems, are supplied.

Source: [publisher methods and Table 3](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.914044/full), cross-checked against the [official Europe PMC XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9388784/fullTextXML).

## Consequences for analysis

- The generic assertion of no missing outcome data does not explain how N/A responses were coded, excluded, or counted. Do not substitute the overall sample size for every domain denominator or infer that every person contributed both ratings.
- Differences between published marginal means can be described arithmetically. Their interpretation as mean within-person differences depends on matched contributing sets. Paired uncertainty additionally requires covariance or individual difference information. Those inputs are unavailable here.
- A younger age band that straddles an adulthood threshold cannot be subtracted wholesale to recover an adult-only analysis. No age-specific interference estimates were found in the checked source material.
- Treat these as results from the study-specific adapted instrument. Do not substitute standard BPI response anchors, scoring rules, or validation claims for undocumented features of this adaptation.

## Availability and status checks

- The public full-text XML has no supplementary-material nodes. The publisher page has no supplementary-material section. The live [Europe PMC bibliographic record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID%3A35990066%20AND%20SRC%3AMED&format=json&resultType=core) flags `hasSuppl=N` and `hasData=N`. The article offers data from the authors, not a linked public dataset. No blank questionnaire or adult-only supplement was located. This is a bounded public-source finding, not proof that the authors hold no additional material.
- No correction, corrigendum, or retraction notice was found on the publisher record or in DOI-specific searches. The XML marks the article as not retracted. The [Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.914044) contains no correction/retraction update relation. These checks do not guarantee that no unlinked notice exists.
- Direct PubMed/PMC HTML access returned a browser-check page during this review; the official Europe PMC record and full-text XML were accessible. A university-repository search result listed an article PDF, without a questionnaire attachment; its live page could not be opened. Neither blocked page was treated as negative proof.

## Release recommendation

Keep the mixed-age, pain-selected scope and unknown item denominators beside any results. Label calculated contrasts as differences between published means. Do not issue paired tests, confidence intervals, clinical thresholds, adult-only estimates, or prevalence claims from these aggregate inputs.
