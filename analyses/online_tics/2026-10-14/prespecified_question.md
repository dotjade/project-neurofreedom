# ONLINE-TICS: bounded time-specific benefit test

Recorded 14 October 2026 at 02:44 UTC, before retrieving the new trial paper, protocol, registry, or outcome tables in this run. This is a local analysis-plan timestamp, not public preregistration.

## Question

In the adult randomized ONLINE-TICS trial (Haas et al. 2022; DOI 10.3390/jcm11010250), what treatment-benefit claim is supported at the prespecified primary endpoint and at later assessments, separately for tic severity, impairment, and GTS-QOL?

## Prediction and disconfirmation

Prediction: the appropriate conclusion will be time- and outcome-specific. A result on tic severity will not by itself establish patient-relevant impairment/QoL benefit, equivalence to face-to-face CBIT, disease modification, or cure. Later nominally significant contrasts may support a narrower hypothesis without rescuing a failed primary endpoint or supplying joint response.

A result that would weaken this prediction would be a prospectively specified, multiplicity-controlled analysis demonstrating consistent primary and later between-group benefit for separately measured tic severity, impairment and GTS-QOL, with adequate missing-data sensitivity and joint outcome data supporting individual concordance. A result that weakens any treatment-benefit claim is a primary between-group interval including no effect, absent/marginal later contrasts after the applicable testing procedure, meaningful attrition sensitivity, or missing patient-relevant benefit.
