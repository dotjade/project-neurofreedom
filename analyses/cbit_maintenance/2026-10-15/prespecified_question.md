# Bounded question recorded before new trial-source extraction

Project NeuroFreedom | 15 October 2026 | Recorded 02:56 UTC

## Question and prior context

Does Wilhelm et al. 2012 (PMC3772729) establish six-month maintenance for the randomized adult-dominant CBIT population, or only a response-selected follow-up population exposed to boosters? The preceding repository update identifies ages 16–69; this is not assumed to be adult-only.

## Prediction

The acute randomized trial supports a different estimand from the maintenance extension. Follow-up will be conditioned on acute improvement, with booster exposure and additional loss to follow-up. Consequently, an observed follow-up maintenance percentage will not identify all-randomized long-term efficacy, off-treatment durability, adult-only efficacy, disease modification, or cure. Separately measured impairment may improve during acute treatment, but this will not establish quality-of-life improvement or maintained impairment benefit without direct follow-up results.

## Disconfirming evidence

The narrow prediction would be weakened by verified six-month assessment of the full randomized population with retained randomized comparison and adequate outcome completeness, or explicit evidence that the quoted maintenance estimate is all-randomized rather than response-conditioned. Verified absence of boosters/continued treatment and a design identifying benefit after withdrawal would weaken the exposure restriction. Direct separately reported follow-up impairment and QoL results would weaken the corresponding missing-outcome limitation. An adult-only subgroup would permit an age-specific extraction but would not retroactively make the overall trial adult-only.

## Plan and limits

Verify paper title, DOI, PMID, PMCID and publication dates from primary sources. Extract eligibility, primary outcome and treatment contrast, randomized/acute/follow-up counts, response definition, follow-up selection and boosters, missingness assumptions, impairment and quality-of-life measurement, adverse events and negative findings. Resolve table/prose differences explicitly. If integer counts suffice, compute deterministic maintenance ascertainment and missing-outcome bounds with denominator-specific labels; add pointwise uncertainty only if its assumptions are stated. Do not reconstruct absent covariances, impute unobserved efficacy or equate bounds with confidence intervals. No participant records, clinical advice, outreach, full-text redistribution, pooling or uncontrolled adult-only inference.

Stop when this one-trial estimand audit and feasible aggregate computation are complete, or required inputs remain unavailable after bounded lawful retrieval. This is a recorded prospective question for this extraction, not preregistration of the original trial or a blinded protocol.
