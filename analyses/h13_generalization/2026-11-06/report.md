# H13 targeted-tic learning audit

6 November 2026. Historical evidence audit, not a new clinical trial, systematic review or treatment recommendation. Original public-source audit; no participant data or intervention.

## Finding and decision

The favorable trained-tic signal remains preliminary. Generalization to untrained tics, global functional benefit, repeated post-treatment durability and the proposed learning mechanism are **not established**. An accessible protocol planned several relevant measurements that the final publication did not report. Their collection/completion and outcomes remain unknown. The four-session follow-up registry is withdrawn with zero enrollment, not a replication awaiting publication.

Retain H13 as an unvalidated generalization/durability hypothesis. Close this bounded audit unless lawful aggregate results or dated amendments explain the reporting and scale gaps. Do not reconstruct untrained-tic effects, pool dissimilar drugs, infer severe-adult benefit, or repeat the same source search.

## Prediction and method

The latest repository plan asks whether targeted-tic improvement supplies broader, lasting learning evidence. The falsifiable audit criterion was that the broad claim requires a separately interpretable untrained/global endpoint and repeated post-treatment follow-up, with harms and missingness disclosed. An isolated trained-tic endpoint cannot meet that criterion. This was a source audit, not a prospectively registered statistical analysis.

## Primary published result

[McGuire et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41386-020-0762-4), [accessible final PDF](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9t62m87q/qt9t62m87q.pdf), PMID 32653895; online July 12, 2020. One 50-mg DCS/placebo dose preceded one hour of HRT by one hour. Follow-up was one week. Models adjusted baseline severity, ADHD and tic medication. Published adjusted means, DCS/placebo:

- Targeted HM/VTS: treatment 5.01/4.93; follow-up 2.41/3.83. Interaction F=13.83, p=.002; follow-up d=1.28 in Table 3 versus 1.30 in the abstract.
- All bothersome HM/VTS: 11.30/11.23 to 6.82/8.66; interaction p=.07, follow-up d=.88.
- Urge frequency interaction p=.02; intensity interaction p=.13. A group main effect is not evidence of different improvement.

No separate untrained-tic, follow-up YGTSS, functional or later durability estimate appears. No direct observation protocol was included according to the discussion. Authors judged three reported DCS-arm adverse effects apparently unrelated. The manuscript acknowledges uncertain mechanism and calls for a multisession trial. Funding included TAA, AAN, APF and NIMH; consulting/royalty relationships were disclosed. [Primary paper](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9t62m87q/qt9t62m87q.pdf)

## Registry, denominators and harms

The [original official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02582515), retrieved through its [API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02582515), records 20 randomized: 9 DCS, 11 placebo. Nine and ten received treatment/completed and contribute the posted primary result; one placebo participant withdrew before treatment. Eligibility was ages 8–17, clinically stable medication, with antipsychotics and several comorbidities excluded. Results were posted April 25, 2019, before the paper.

Posted targeted-tic change means are 1.22 (SD .62) and .70 (.54), n=9/10. The registry alternates summed/averaged wording and gives a 0–8 range while calling the two-tic score an average. Do not silently rescale these or treat them as the paper's adjusted estimates.

Systematic side-effect review occurred immediately after treatment and one week later. Other adverse events affected 3/9 versus 0/10: one each drowsiness, difficulty falling asleep and sore throat. Serious events and deaths were zero in both arms. Short exposure and tiny denominators cannot establish rare, cumulative or combination-treatment safety. The selected aggregate fields are saved separately for verification.

## Protocol/SAP reconciliation

[Official protocol/SAP](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/15/NCT02582515/Prot_SAP_000.pdf), updated October 24, 2016, uploaded March 2019:

- PDF page 6 planned Visit-3 YGTSS, CGI improvement, video observation and parent/child tic questionnaires. Pages 6–7 planned a Weather Prediction learning task.
- Page 11 specified averaged two-tic severity in repeated-measures ANOVA across three visits, with ADHD and alpha-agonist covariates. The published analysis instead describes two repeated time points with baseline severity adjustment.
- Page 10 says expected HRT-related subjective distress would not be classified as an adverse event unless the child/parent specifically described it as one.

These are planned-versus-reported differences, not proof that measures were collected, concealed or null. The protocol is mid-study and was posted after completion; it cannot establish original prospective specification or the absence of later amendments. Its ascertainment rule also means the event count is not a complete measure of treatment discomfort. Public aggregate results, actual completion counts and dated amendments would be needed to resolve these gaps.

## Replication and counterevidence

The [four-session follow-up NCT04357951](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04357951) is WITHDRAWN for low enrollment, actual enrollment zero, no results; last posted April 14, 2023. Its estimated 2024 completion date is not a completed-study result. The planned primary endpoint again concerned targeted tics one week after treatment. It supplies neither observed replication nor long-term durability.

[Lemmon et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4454293/), DOI 10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2015.02.002, randomized 24 young people to riluzole (10), D-serine (9) or placebo (5), with six treatment weeks and two taper weeks. The abstract reports one dropout. Mean TTS percentage improvements were 38%, 25% and 34%; riluzole/placebo p=.85 and D-serine/placebo p=.69. Combined tic-plus-impairment differences were also nonsignificant. This does not validate generic glutamate modulation, but it is not a DCS-plus-HRT test and cannot prove DCS has no direct effect. Current full-text access was incomplete; detailed harms were not freshly re-extracted.

[Storch et al. 2016](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2529151), DOI 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2016.1128, randomized 142 youth with OCD to CBT augmented with DCS or placebo. The primary symptom trajectories did not differ significantly. This is a caution against promoting an encouraging pilot, not a tic-treatment replication or proof of identical mechanisms.

[Günthner et al. 2016](https://www.win.ox.ac.uk/publication/632370), DOI 10.1177/0269881116658988: the institutional primary abstract describes 54 healthy volunteers randomized to 250-mg DCS/placebo and a motor-sequence task, with no faster or greater learning. Different dose, population and task prevent direct transfer to HRT. It limits a universal motor-learning-enhancement claim; it does not identify the tic trial's mechanism. Abstract-only extraction here.

## Interpretation and next bounded action

Generalization needs an endpoint that excludes practiced targets, or a clearly defined whole-person outcome. A total containing the targets cannot identify the untrained component. Even a significant total-score result would not solve that identification problem. Separately fitted adjusted means cannot safely be subtracted to invent an untrained-tic treatment effect or confidence interval without compatible scoring, populations, model terms and covariance.

A one-week outcome during continued skill practice is neither long-term durability nor treatment-free disease modification. An association with urge change would not by itself identify extinction, reversal learning or mediation. No shared mechanism should be presumed from the broad label glutamatergic.

New work should first map which H18 behavioral-learning claims have direct randomized mechanism/generalization evidence using existing public aggregate reports, without repeating the already completed adult CBIT maintenance/function audits. For H13 specifically, reopen only for public missing-outcome summaries, score reconciliation, dated amendments or an actual replicated multisession result. Persistent severe adults, pain, participation and co-occurring conditions remain distinct evidence needs. No owner action or clinical experiment is required.
