# Prospective comparison plan

Recorded 2026-11-13T03:06:11.554704+00:00 before this run’s primary-source extraction, after receiving candidate summaries. This is not a blinded preregistration.

Question: Do three candidate historical reports provide interchangeable evidence about observed tic frequency, clinician tic severity/impairment and participant-described burden, or do apparent differences follow from distinct constructs, contexts and cohorts?

Prediction: Same-instrument severity/impairment association and patient-described burden results need not conflict. Direct calibration, agreement or validated prediction of patient burden from tic counts would disconfirm this limited prediction. Mere nonsignificance will not be interpreted as equivalence or absence of association.

Method: Check source dates/IDs and prior repository coverage. Extract aggregate denominators, measurement context, estimates/uncertainty and limitations from three primary reports. Establish cohort links before considering independence. Do not pool different correlations, conditions or repeated assessments, infer individual biology, rerun H18 simulations or collect participant data. Preserve favorable and contrary evidence and abstract/full-text discrepancies.

Outputs: Original source-linked evidence matrix, cohort map, reproducible consistency/coverage checks and a bounded synthesis. No new effect estimate is required if outcomes are incomparable; record why. Stop after three reports and state access gaps. H18 remains closed unless the named invariance/calibration prerequisite is actually met.
