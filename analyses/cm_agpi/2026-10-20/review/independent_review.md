# Independent aggregate-methods review

Checked 2026-10-20 UTC. No participant rows, raw signals, full-paper copies, or identifying clinical profiles retained.

## Paper: bounded interpretation

Six adults (19–32) were implanted; electrophysiology excluded one, leaving five. Methods/Figure 1 specify three initial unstimulated months, whereas the abstract says nine recording visits: preserve that discrepancy. Contrasts used rest, cued hand movements, and free tics; tic subtypes were pooled. This observational physiology analysis does not establish intervention efficacy, prospective forecasting, or pediatric applicability.

Features were averaged across participants; Wilcoxon/Kruskal–Wallis tests compared group-level time-frequency features with Bonferroni correction. The independent observation unit and treatment of repeated visits/overlapping epochs are insufficiently explicit for verifying population-level uncertainty. Small n alone does not establish statistical invalidity.

Null findings concern RMS differences, several beta/low-gamma power/coherence comparisons, and beta phase comparisons. Results nevertheless report significant low-gamma interhemispheric phase features; the Discussion’s broader negative statement conflicts. Avoid “all higher-frequency effects absent.” Phase lead and coherence support association/directional hypotheses, not a proven causal tic generator. No prospectively validated forecast, symptom-benefit comparison, or transition from urge to tic is demonstrated. Adult refractory surgical sampling and electrode-placement exclusion limit transportability. Full test denominators, nesting, correction families, and sensitivity analyses should precede stronger inference.

Source: [Lowor et al., full article](https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mds.70043), Methods: Participants, Postoperative Visits, Data Analysis; Results: Electrophysiology, Phase Synchrony; Discussion. [PubMed identity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40977255/).

## Registry denominator and state

The directly retrieved registry API lists actual enrollment eight, eligibility 18–85, single-group unmasked device feasibility, and a nine-month YGTSS Total Tic Score outcome. Results were first posted 2026-09-25. This describes a broader trial and endpoint, not an automatic extension of the paper’s recording analysis. The registry excludes previous DBS, including explanted systems; that is relevant to cohort provenance, but does not resolve overlap across every later publication.
