# Preregistered synthetic paired-outcome discrimination test

Recorded 2026-10-19T03:07:06Z before writing/executing analysis code or generating inputs/results. This local prospective methods plan is not an externally registered clinical protocol. Its SHA-256 is recorded separately before execution.

## Question, predictions and failure criteria

Can observing paired tic and pain-interference changes distinguish constructed worlds which have exactly identical tic records? Tic-only data must be identical across all worlds. Direct complete paired pain measurement should distinguish a constant additive pain benefit (A) from zero mean pain benefit (B), and distinguish heterogeneous cancellation (C) from B through the joint tic–pain covariance even though B and C share zero mean pain improvement. These are algebraically imposed toy mechanisms, not proposed patient biology. If tic-only records differ, the construction fails. If noiseless complete pairing fails to recover the specified means or cross-moments, the code fails. If repeated independent zero-mean measurement error or positive-probability missingness generates material systematic bias in the declared corrected raw moments, the expected observation model is disconfirmed for this implementation; report rather than tune settings.

A deliberate negative control observes only X=-1. A and C then have identical complete observed records despite different full-population pain means. Direct measurement alone must not resolve this nonidentification. Any claim that it does is a failed inference. No binary response labels or clinical cutoffs are used.

## Fixed synthetic population and mechanisms

Use N=400 artificial units, X=-1 for 200 and X=+1 for 200. Within each stratum draw 100 independent U,V uniform(-1,1) pairs and append their negatives, ensuring zero stratum means. Seed 20261019, Python standard-library random.Random. All baseline outcomes are 5 arbitrary units. Positive change means baseline minus follow-up (improvement). Tic change T=2+0.4X+0.2U in all worlds. Pain change A=1+0.4X+0.2V; B=0.4X+0.2V; C=A-2*I(X=+1). These are finite-population descriptive changes with no randomized treatment or causal effect estimand. Shared U,V preserve matched worlds; no empirically fitted parameters or real records.

Target full-population means: T=2, pain A=1, B=C=0. Joint target: population covariance of T and P, defined using divisor N, and raw cross-moment E[TP]. Because finite U,V products need not average zero, calculate covariance truth from complete noiseless constructed records, not only asymptotic formulas. B and C also differ in pain marginal variance; joint covariance is informative but is not the only possible discriminator. C versus B has exact covariance difference -0.4 and cross-moment difference -0.4, while A versus B has covariance difference zero and pain-mean difference 1. A versus C pain mean differs by 1.

## Observation, error and missingness

Run 200 matched observation replicates, independent seeds 20262019+r for r=0..199. Add independent uniform(-0.25,0.25) error to each of tic baseline, tic follow-up, pain baseline and pain follow-up. Reuse errors and observation uniforms across worlds. Error is independent between domains/timepoints and of latent variables and missingness. Changes are differences of noisy visits. Latent and measured scores remain within [0,10] by construction; do not clip. Measurement error can obscure individual paired changes; its independent zero means preserve expected means and raw cross-moments, not every realized covariance or correlation. Correlations and attenuation corrections are outside scope.

Designs: complete p=1; MCAR p=0.6; observed-X MAR p=0.8 when X=-1 and 0.3 when X=+1; support-loss p=1 for X=-1 and 0 for X=+1. A unit has both domains and both visits observed, or neither; X and N are known for all units. Observation probabilities are known simulation inputs, not estimated from data. This unusually explicit pairing assumption is a limit. MAR missingness depends on observed X only. Support loss violates positivity and admits no inverse-probability full-population recovery.

For complete, MCAR and MAR, report complete-case mean/covariance and Horvitz–Thompson raw moments: sum(R*T/p)/N, sum(R*P/p)/N, sum(R*T*P/p)/N. Derived HT plug-in covariance is cross-moment minus product of estimated means and is not promised unbiased. Report its bias separately. Use no weights for p=0; report full-population weighted estimands unavailable under support loss. The selected-subpopulation descriptive estimates remain reportable.

## Outputs, checks and decision

Save generated finite-population input CSV, source, exact seeds/configuration, 2,400 replicate summaries (200*4*3), summary JSON, tests, execution provenance and report. Report mean estimation error and Monte Carlo standard error (sample SD of replicate errors/sqrt(200)); these characterize simulation repeatability, not clinical uncertainty. A preregistered diagnostic flags |mean error|>4 Monte Carlo SE plus 1e-10 for raw moments only, retaining all flags without parameter adjustment. No hypothesis test, design ranking, power or sample-size sufficiency is inferred. Tests check exact matched tic data, noiseless algebra, score bounds, observation probabilities, missingness counts, negative-control identity, reproducibility, and independent moment calculations. Optional independent arithmetic review is separate from clinical validation.

The intended result is a bounded design lesson: direct jointly measured outcomes can distinguish some stipulated worlds, conditional on observation assumptions; unsupported strata or invalid errors can defeat identification. Stop after one fixed run and verification. Do not tune or expand scenarios to obtain a preferred result.
