# Independent review of the revised Marras endpoint boundary

18 October 2026. This reviews the endpoint-status/access interpretation, not the inaccessible primary trial tables or clinical effectiveness.

## Result

The revised audit uses the correct distinction: **a pain-related measurement is corroborated by a lawful secondary review; its complete primary definition and pain-benefit interpretation remain unverified**. Do not classify the endpoint as absent. Do not infer analgesic benefit, pain inefficacy, equivalence, or a pain-interference outcome.

The reviewer independently located the NIHR/NCBI review lead, then inspected the [official NIHR PDF](https://njl-admin.nihr.ac.uk/document/download/2003385) in memory through an ordinary HTTP 200 response. No paper copy was saved. Printed page 210 places treated-tic-area pain in a Safety subsection, says the scale was not reported, and describes the comparison as inconclusive. Figure 216 on printed page 211 identifies Marras 2001 and a distinct pain comparison. Thus secondary corroboration is stronger than the original unverified upload snippet, without supplying the missing primary construct or establishing treatment benefit. The review is not another trial or an independent replication.

## Checks against the revised deliverables

- Report and structured extraction both retain secondary corroboration and no endpoint-absence conclusion.
- The initial absence prediction is marked weakened, not confirmed.
- Accepted pain scale, recall window, interference construct, outcome-specific denominator, effect estimate, interval and p-value remain unset in the primary extraction.
- The secondary review's standardized effect is not imported as a primary estimate or computational input.
- The complete-primary-methods gate remains unmet; no new numerical pain analysis is claimed.
- Crossover pairing, treatment-period/sequence issues, selection and adult-only applicability remain unresolved rather than filled from the abstract's overall completer count.

The reviewer did not independently reproduce every publisher/metadata access attempt, and did not inspect the inaccessible original tables. Original full methods/tables and relevant supplements remain the exact prerequisite for a primary pain-benefit reanalysis. This review does not authorize outreach, payment, participant access or intervention.
