# H18 mechanism-to-decision evidence map

Research date: 7 November 2026. Plan recorded after repository and title/abstract discovery, before full primary-methods extraction. This is a bounded evidence audit, not prospectively registered research or an exhaustive systematic review.

## Question and prediction

Do direct behavioral component/mechanism experiments establish a transferable rule for selecting behavioral components according to urge habituation, contextual triggers, or motor-learning characteristics, with incremental patient-centered benefit over standard CBIT?

Prediction: efficacy of multicomponent CBIT and prognostic associations will exceed direct evidence for a personalized allocation rule. Mean urge reduction will not be shown to be a necessary or sufficient mediator. Comparator equivalence, suppression in one laboratory context and clinical generalization will need distinct estimands.

## Scope and fixed search

Read latest repository README, protocol, status, public journal README, H18 and prior adult maintenance/functioning and H13 records. Do not rerun those audits. Search primary studies about urge habituation, competing-response/component manipulations, randomized ERP versus HRT and transfer to untrained contexts/tics. Retain older supportive and contradictory studies. Inspect public aggregate sources only. No participant data, intervention, contact or treatment advice.

For each eligible report record population, assignment, actually manipulated variable, measured mediator, timing, comparator, causal contrast, transfer endpoint, harms/effort ascertainment, cohort dependence, limits and what the comparison can identify. Separate randomized treatment packages, randomized/counterbalanced laboratory conditions, nonrandomized mediator analyses and predictive studies.

## Decision rule

A personalized-selection claim requires a randomized model-guided allocation strategy or a prospectively tested treatment-by-pre-treatment-characteristic contrast with adequate precision, followed by independent validation. Generalization needs an explicit untrained tic/context endpoint; global severity alone does not isolate transfer. Mechanism mediation requires temporal and confounding assumptions, not a response association. Do not pool incompatible designs or derive missing precision.

Stop this map after a bounded set of direct reports and deliberate counterevidence search. If no compatible treatment-selection comparison is verified, keep H18 proposed and leave one feasible next design/identifiability task. Report source access limits and evidence gaps without claiming nonexistence of studies.
