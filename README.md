# Project NeuroFreedom

## Initial evidence portfolio

8 October 2026 | Version 0.1

A research program toward a safe, lasting and scientifically validated cure for Tourette syndrome. Its immediate output is a testable, source-linked research agenda. It does not claim a cure has been found, that every relevant paper has been reviewed, or that proposed mechanisms are established treatment targets.

**Research priority:** understand sustained developmental improvement and remission using consistent definitions, then test whether proposed biological mechanisms predict or cause durable benefit. Sleep-related changes are a mechanistic clue, but tics can persist during sleep, so sleep does not establish a universal off-switch.

**Clinical boundary:** this portfolio is for research planning and clinician discussion. It contains no individual treatment prescription and no protocol for experimenting on a child. Wellbeing, voluntary participation and appropriate clinical care take priority over data collection.

The initial portfolio is a targeted evidence map. Search coverage, access limitations, study-level uncertainty and contradictions must remain visible. Primary papers, guidance, registries, preprints and hypotheses are distinguished. A promising laboratory finding, retrospective association or symptomatic benefit is not evidence of cure.

## Privacy and reuse

The intended GitHub repository is private. No patient-specific medical information, family identifiers, images, diary entries or genetic data belong here. Store original evidence summaries and source identifiers. Do not upload unlicensed full-text papers or private community content. A private repository is not a clinical-data system.

## How to continue

Read the protocol and latest status first. Verify the latest source and repository version before changing claims. Add evidence with provenance and retain contradictory and negative findings. Record what was actually done, what is only proposed, and what is blocked. Do not claim data access, an analysis, collaboration or scheduled research is active until verified.
