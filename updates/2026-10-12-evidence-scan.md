## Result

One newly indexed, relevant preprint was verified: a 50-person comparison of CBIT plus 1 Hz rTMS, cTBS or sham in ages 12–21. It was first posted on 1 October and entered PubMed's history on 11 October, so it is new to this scan rather than newly published today. It remains unreviewed evidence. Neural target engagement and improvement across all arms do not demonstrate added clinical benefit, durable disease modification or efficacy in persistent severe adult illness. See the [source and denominator audit](2026-10-12-cbit-tms-extraction.md) and [separate preprint record](2026-10-12-preprint-evidence.jsonl).

No newly first-published peer-reviewed primary tic study, in-window ClinicalTrials.gov update/result, or material FDA/EMA/MHRA target-therapy safety notice was verified in this bounded check. An older adult Brazilian registry result was also found: the global symptom endpoint was nonsignificant and one participant withdrew with headache. It is explicitly a backlog finding with reporting limitations, not a new October trial result. The [registry records](2026-10-12-registry-evidence.jsonl) remain separate from both preprints and peer-reviewed primary-study evidence.

These findings justify source reconciliation before promoting the stimulation hypothesis. They do not warrant a treatment recommendation or change to the all-age/adult-burden mission. No participant records, family information, outreach, enrollment, purchase or medical intervention were involved.

Keep the preprint as preliminary neural-target evidence with an unresolved clinical contrast. The next useful step is lawful full-text and protocol-amendment reconciliation, rather than calculating an effect from ambiguous fields. The separate adult sensory-gating priority remains appropriate; no unchanged MNS analysis or closed generic cohort search was repeated.

The full preprint was not accessible through the inspected web routes. The available official abstract, version/license metadata, current aggregate registry record and published protocol support the limited extraction above. No full paper or participant file is included in the repository-ready deliverables. These searches are not exhaustive systematic review, complete registry-history reconstruction or clinical peer review; late indexing, date-only metadata, dynamic API updates, inaccessible pages and unretrieved full methods remain material limits.
