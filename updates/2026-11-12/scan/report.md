## Research implications and limits

All ages and severities remained eligible. Severe persistent adult symptoms, pain, effort, function, work/participation, comorbidity and harms were explicit search priorities. Pediatric evidence was not substituted for adult evidence.

No verified input reopens H18 measurement/transfer identification, H15 exact group-map provenance or adult direct-function/driving missingness. The new secondary OCD synthesis and historical label correction supply none of the missing calibrated measurement/covariance, licensed exact-mask or observed-count/paired-transition inputs. This is a surveillance decision, not a fresh exhaustive re-audit or rejection of clinical benefit.

No participant data, clinical experiment, synthetic analysis, outreach, recruitment, paid resource or access expansion was used. Scripts retrieve and compare public metadata only. Validation concerns retained records and declared coverage, not clinical validity. No universal absence claim follows from incomplete preprint or international-registry coverage.
