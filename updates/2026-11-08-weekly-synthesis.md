# Project NeuroFreedom weekly synthesis

8 November 2026 | Fifth weekly synthesis | Review window: 1–8 November 2026

## Decision

**Keep clinical function and burden first; advance measurement identification before personalized prediction.** The week sharpened what the available evidence can support, rather than producing a new treatment result. H18 remains a proposed research hypothesis. H15's group-file integrity gate passed, but its spatial-replication gate did not. H13's trained-tic signal remains preliminary and does not establish untrained-tic or durable functional benefit. None of these findings warrants a treatment change, target ranking, disease-modification claim or cure claim.

This is an append-only synthesis of completed work, not another publication search. November 1's daily, weekly and monthly findings form the starting baseline; the November 2–8 audits are the subsequent advances. The reviewed public repository snapshot is [c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/commit/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1). The question remains global and all-age Tourette/chronic tic research, explicitly including persistent severe adult symptoms, pain, effort, participation, co-occurring conditions and unmet needs. A reduction in observed tics is not interchangeable with improvement in these outcomes.

## What actually changed

### 1. New publication and safety evidence stayed narrow

Eight dated daily reports and their scan summaries are present for November 1–8. They verified **one new peer-reviewed journal measurement publication**, Wu et al., published November 6 and reviewed November 7. The publisher reports an April 19 preprint: it is not first public disclosure, an independent replication or a treatment study. No qualifying new preprint or newly posted trial result was verified in these bounded scans. Registry date changes were administrative, not clinical outcomes. Daily retrieval counts overlap and must not be summed into a weekly unique-study count.

The [Wu primary paper](https://ai.jmir.org/2026/1/e98782) reports 49/55 correct predictions for selected pediatric ward-video clips. Those are clips, not 55 independent patients. Participant-disjoint splitting is a strength, but exact test/validation patient allocation, correlated-clip uncertainty, selected controls and lack of independent home/held-out-center validation limit transport. Three comparator accuracies do not reconcile with integer counts out of the stated common 55 clips. The audit flags this without inventing a corrected denominator. Retain preliminary measurement evidence; reject standalone diagnosis, deployment or clinical-benefit claims. [Extraction](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/updates/2026-11-07/scan/new-evidence.jsonl).

The November 1 safety review identified a [Singapore HSA recall](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/announcements/recall-of-tetrabenazine-25mg-112-s-sun-italy/) for one tetrabenazine product batch, DNG1035A, after black spots were reported. Its recall action was dated October 27 and the notice displayed November 1. This is a product/batch-specific quality issue, not a class-wide pharmacological hazard or a Tourette-specific result. Later bounded checks verified no new watched-treatment warning; that is not proof of safety. The already-retracted review [PMID 41503301](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41503301/) is excluded from substantive support. November 8's clarification was not a newly occurring retraction.

### 2. Adult function was directly measured, but stronger conclusions still fail

The November 1 baseline audit already rejected the claim that CANNA-TICS function was merely inferred from tic scores: direct randomized function/QoL contrasts exist. All five focal 95% intervals include zero, so neither established superiority nor no worthwhile benefit follows. For example, normalized GTS-QoL was reported as −4.13 [−8.65, 0.39]. The VAS arm-mean/contrast mismatch remains unresolved. [Baseline analysis](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/canna_function/2026-11-01/report.md); [primary trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psychres.2023.115135).

The November 2 audit partly resolved documentation: function instruments and exploratory analysis status were specified. The protocol's no-imputation statement applies to its additional mixed model, not every analysis. The paper's qualitative sensitivity assertion is retained as counterevidence to a blanket instability claim. However, observed endpoint denominators, numerical sensitivity contrasts, VAS reconciliation and dated analysis-plan history remain unavailable. An institutional attachment was readable but contained an unrelated paper; HTTP success and a matching checksum did not establish source identity. [Documentation audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/canna_documentation/2026-11-02/report.md).

The November 3 same-trial driving audit retained the favorable reported estimate, +0.17 [−0.08, 0.43], but the [protocol](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2020.575826) gated confirmatory noninferiority on primary superiority, which did not pass. Clearing a −0.32 numerical margin cannot restore that failed hierarchy. Percentage-compatible denominators and transition counts are not fully reconciled; they do not establish a paper error. Computerized fitness is not road-crash safety or individual driving clearance. [Driving audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/canna_driving/2026-11-03/report.md); [primary substudy](https://doi.org/10.1089/can.2023.0114).

**Decision:** close these fixed documentation searches pending the exact lawful aggregate tables/methods. Keep direct adult function, missingness and harms high priority without promoting an endocannabinoid selector or counting same-trial reports as replication.

### 3. Network work passed a file check, not a biological test

November 4 verified three explicitly group-only, publicly licensed maps, hashes and matching header grids from the [Baldermann study's](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55242-6) linked archive. This was genuine deterministic artifact analysis. It did not reproduce the full outcome pipeline, which needs unavailable individual inputs and external dependencies. Prepublication archive/final-paper mapping is unresolved. Matching headers do not prove alignment, specificity or target superiority. [Feasibility audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/network_feasibility/2026-11-04/report.md).

November 5 could not establish the exact custom atlas/table, appended CON/SCAN masks, reuse terms and version/space mapping. A generic atlas is not an acceptable substitute. A clearly synthetic calculation showed balanced accuracy rising from 0.6000 to 0.6990 when only empty background was added, while coverage stayed 0.4. This justifies requiring a common domain; it does not show the published result was biased. Coverage and cover ratio are algebraically related, not independent corroboration. [Provenance and synthetic audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/network_provenance/2026-11-05/report.md).

**Decision:** pause H15 spatial replication until exact safe inputs and a prespecified common domain/spatial null are available. Preserve favorable network associations alongside the historical randomized SMA trial that did not demonstrate active-versus-sham superiority. Neither null superiority nor missing inputs disproves network biology.

### 4. Learning signals do not yet establish generalized, durable benefit

November 6 reconciled [McGuire's DCS-plus-HRT pilot](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41386-020-0762-4), its registry and protocol. The trained-tic signal at one week remains supported. A total that includes trained targets cannot identify improvement in untrained tics; the nonsignificant broader interaction is not proof of no benefit. Planned broader measures absent from the final paper have unknown collection status and outcomes. The [four-session follow-up](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04357951) is withdrawn with zero enrollment, not an observed replication. Separate negative D-serine/riluzole evidence is relevant counterevidence, not a license to pool unlike interventions. [H13 audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/h13_generalization/2026-11-06/report.md).

November 7 separated whole-CBIT efficacy, global-urge mediation, baseline moderation, randomized reward manipulation and transfer. The inspected global-urge indirect-effect intervals include zero; this weakens a demonstrated global-urge mediation claim without proving no mediation. A baseline-urge interaction concerns the CBIT package versus supportive therapy, not selection between components. Three secondary papers reuse the original two CBIT trials.

The [randomized reward/gameplay study](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.29875) supports trained suppression and a short observed-tic contrast; broader severity contrasts did not establish corresponding incremental benefit. Carryover, joint manipulation, registration timing and endpoint/harms limits remain. Positive ERP habituation observations and controlled evidence against increased new bothersome tics are retained. They neither validate a universal mechanism nor establish positive untreated-tic transfer. [H18 source map](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/h18_mechanism/2026-11-07/report.md).

**Decision:** retain H13 and H18 as unvalidated extensions. Do not convert a task effect, treatment moderator, absence of demonstrated substitution or short follow-up into a personalized component rule or durable everyday function.

### 5. The completed synthetic test identifies the next prerequisite

November 8's [H18 experiment](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/h18_identifiability/2026-11-08/report.md) used invented contributions, not observations from people. Under a known, calibrated and invariant measurement model, the two artificial contributions are identifiable. With an unknown reporting shift, distinct latent explanations can generate the exact same observed distribution. Repetition cannot resolve that ambiguity.

In the prespecified hidden-shift case with strong loading and eight repeats, the misspecified model confidently selected the wrong population explanation in 94.58% of 4,000 simulated datasets. This is neither individual classification accuracy nor an estimate of real instrument bias. Treating repeated rows as independent also caused undercoverage. Context transfer does not automatically identify mechanism when its coefficients are unknown.

The package records 14 measurement and six transfer scenarios, 4,000 datasets each, seed 20261108, a byte-identical rerun and 261 second-calculation checks. These are recorded methods checks, not independent clinical validation. The two hidden-shift cases both have eight repeats, so they do not estimate the effect of increasing repetition under hidden shift.

**Fresh weekly verification:** the [separate implementation audit](2026-11-08-weekly/computation-audit/report.md) passed 352/352 new assertions and reran the 261/261 saved checks with byte-identical check output. An independent scalar reconstruction reproduced 302/4,000 correct generation-dominant labels in the weak-loading, single-repeat scenario and 3,783/4,000 wrong control-dominant labels in the strong-loading, eight-repeat hidden-shift scenario. It used the same documented random streams to verify implementation, not to claim independent random replication. This satisfies the weekly requirement to complete or verify one bounded analysis without presenting the daily simulation as a second new finding. No numerical or code correction was required. The plan freeze is locally recorded, not independently time-certified preregistration. The audit adds no scenario, clinical cohort, measurement validation or biological evidence; it rejects this unanchored mechanism assignment, not H18 globally. The original prediction and negative results remain unchanged.

**Decision:** stop this fixed simulation. The next requirement is a defensible external measurement anchor and explicit invariance/transfer assumptions, not a more complicated predictor.

## What to reject, retain and pause

- **Reject:** new retrieval equals new publication; scheduled work equals completed work; multiple papers from one cohort equal independent replication.
- **Reject:** split-half reliability establishes retest or difference-score reliability. November 1's force-matching arithmetic changed the aggregate contrast by 0.12 N across extraction windows, without supplying covariance or an inferential interaction. [Audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/c9d1bae55750e460768236ced30f29f54721dbb1/analyses/force_matching_reliability/2026-11-01/report.md).
- **Reject:** a favorable exploratory interval overrides a failed confirmatory hierarchy; nonsignificance proves equivalence, safety or zero benefit.
- **Reject:** readable attachments, matching hashes, matching grids or synthetic recovery establish scientific identity, spatial specificity or a human mechanism.
- **Retain:** favorable functional, trained-tic, reward-component and network signals with their exact uncertainty and counterevidence. Missing sources are access/provenance failures, not negative biological results.
- **Pause:** repeated searches of closed CANNA-TICS, force-matching, H13 and H15 questions until their named missing inputs change. Do not turn every unresolved result into another open-ended audit.

## Next safe experiment: can an external anchor distinguish the mechanisms?

**Owner and cap:** next authorized research run; one public-source audit, at most 90 minutes and six primary reports, zero paid compute. This is a proposed cap, not a claim that the audit has run. No outreach, participant files, symptom/video collection, clinical intervention or public model deployment.

**Question:** does existing participant-free measurement evidence offer a defensible anchor for separating urge-generation change from action-control change, rather than merely restating tic reduction?

**Prespecified prediction:** repeatability or correlation alone will not identify the two contributions. A useful anchor must justify what construct is measured and address systematic reporting change, timing and context. Evidence meeting those requirements would weaken this pessimistic prediction.

**Artifact and observable:** create one source-linked measurement-adequacy matrix. For each source record population, global versus tic-specific construct, comparator, repeat-session/absolute agreement and uncertainty, treatment/context invariance, temporal ordering, participant clustering, missingness and a transfer estimand. Explicitly distinguish an observed-tic measure from an external anchor for the latent mechanism. Record a concrete alternative explanation for every proposed anchor.

**Decision rules:**
- **Advance only to design preparation** if a compatible public source supplies a defensible anchor plus sufficient aggregate information to state a discriminating test and a meaningful failure criterion. Specify the identifying assumption that remains untested. Do not declare causal mediation or individual personalization validated.
- **Reject an anchor for this use** if it is circular, changes construct across contexts, conflates trained and untrained outcomes, relies on incompatible populations or lacks the required identifying restriction.
- **Inconclusive/stop** if the necessary evidence is absent, inaccessible or too imprecise. Preserve the mechanism as unidentified, name the exact missing aggregate quantity and close the audit. Do not fill the gap with personal data or another simulation of the same ambiguity.

Any later human matching strategy would require an independent clinical institution, ethics/consent, appropriate data governance, external validation and an equally intensive randomized strategy comparison that measures function, effort, harms and durable transfer. None is initiated.
