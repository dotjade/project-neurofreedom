## Essential corrections and omissions to avoid

1. October29 completed all36 safe gene memberships. October28 'finish gene table' is historical, not an outstanding task. Counts are threshold membership, not biological specificity or independent replication. BioGRID nonconvergence and negative component burden remain.
2. C2's initial 'highest-priority public computation' is conditional: public availability was reported, not verified acquisition/license. Monthly should explicitly supersede unconditional wording without pretending the computation ran.
3. New adult force attenuation remains concurrent association with unresolved formal contrast and temporal reliability. Split-half .94/.86 is not repeat-session reliability, difference reliability or clinical prediction. Close its repetitive synthetic branch pending the exact lawful aggregate prerequisites.
4. October31 mixed-diagnosis HRH3 bulk-expression pilot does not promote H05. Histamine expression, receptor activity and subgroup rescue are distinct.
5. October28 negative VMAT2 intervals constrain only chosen pediatric implementations and post-hoc illustrative thresholds, not all dopamine biology. Opposing adult DBS trials must remain separate, not averaged into pediatric medication.
6. Retire unsupported inferential bridges, not all20 mechanisms wholesale. Absence of accessible sufficient statistics is a prerequisite gap, not falsification.
7. Distinguish exploratory methods checks from empirical discoveries. The synthesis's synthetic causal counterexample and later force-contrast examples identify limitations by construction, not patient biology. Test counts are implementation checks, not scientific replications.
8. No new global safety scan or exhaustive novelty review performed by this review. Daily safety notices are batch/product specific; do not translate them into class rejection.
