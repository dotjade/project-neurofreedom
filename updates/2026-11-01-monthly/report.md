## Competing causal models and retired inferences

The October 28 priority model, input variability interacting with retuning lag, remains **a hypothesis needing reliable prospective measurements**, not the leading proven biology. Reaching/severity correlations and fitted learning-parameter differences were null; predictive coding and binding remain unresolved alternatives. Event probability versus identity and heterogeneous development converging on a controllable state remain distinct models. No current lawful aggregate input permits their proposed human tests. No novelty claim follows from arranging these existing ideas.

Retire the following inferences, not all underlying biological possibilities:

- A target-sensitive drug or device response identifies a universal upstream chemical defect. Pediatric VMAT2 failures, conflicting severe-adult GPi trials and the negative SMA trial constrain that leap. Their absence of positive results is not proof against every dopamine/network mechanism.
- Every one of the 36 gene-union members is independently tic-specific or a treatment target. The completed membership partition is 3 both-disorder, 7 CTD-not-OCD, 9 OCD-not-CTD and 17 combined-only; analysis thresholds do not establish exclusivity, causal effect or independent replication. PCNet convergence did not replicate in BioGRID.
- A sensory-attenuation association is a prospective tic trigger. Formal adult group-by-condition estimates and safe full methods remain missing; split-half coefficients .94/.86 do not establish repeat-session reliability. The descriptive .12 N window shift is not a clinical effect or formal interaction.
- Tic improvement automatically establishes function, pain or quality-of-life benefit; selected maintenance establishes treatment-free durability; same-trial reports are independent replications; pooled alpha supplies subgroup invariance. These bridges failed the reviewed evidence or remain untested.
- A null p value proves no worthwhile benefit. Inconclusive function contrasts, negative implementation trials and missing documentation require precision and context, not universal dismissal. Conversely, post hoc subgroup hints do not repair a failed primary test.

H10 and H11 use outputs from the same six-TS/six-control postmortem study, not two independent causal replications. The October30 pediatric immune association and multiplicity-corrected vitamin nulls do not supply that missing adult causal replication.

Preserve the failed and uncertain findings alongside positive symptomatic evidence. DLPFC molecular evidence remains **preprint**, animal/cell rescue remains **preclinical**, registry entries remain **plans/metadata**, and the project's simulations remain **synthetic methods**. No anecdote supplies a target-validation claim.
