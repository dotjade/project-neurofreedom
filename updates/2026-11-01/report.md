## Bounded original analysis

**Prediction:** a clinically interpretable between-person force-matching measure needs a stable, understood indirect baseline and reproducibility across sessions. Similar within-session means or high internal consistency alone should not establish those properties.

**Methods:** audit McNaughton et al., [DOI 10.3758/s13428-022-01954-w](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-022-01954-w), online August 24, 2022, issue 2023. Inspect lawful primary methods, aggregate tables, trial-order analysis and coefficient definitions. Compare an adjacent pinch-retest study only for construct compatibility. Calculate equal-force direct-minus-slider contrasts from the same published table at two extraction windows. Code, rounded inputs, outputs and limitations are versioned with the audit. No participant files, contact, intervention or paid compute were used.

**Published findings:** 138 mostly young adults completed one session. Eight-repetition split-half coefficients were 0.94 direct and 0.86 slider, not separate-day retest estimates or reliability of the condition difference. The reported mean whole-session slope was 0.0004 N per trial. This null trend counts against a large common monotonic shift, but combines counterbalanced condition blocks and does not isolate indirect learning. A different pinch task's retest coefficients cannot fill that gap. Unresolved dispersion reporting in that adjacent source is retained conditionally, without inventing corrected values.

**Executed result:** equal-weight contrasts were 0.8925 N at 2–2.5 seconds and 0.7725 N at 2.5–3 seconds: a decrease of 0.1200 N, about 13.45%. The change decomposes into a 0.025 N decrease in direct error and a 0.095 N increase in slider error. A direct plateau does not imply a stable condition contrast. **Uncertainty:** inputs are rounded. The conditional worst-case rounding bound is 0.02 N, not a sampling confidence interval. Cross-condition and cross-window covariance is unavailable, so no inferential interval or significance claim is produced. Single-device young-adult findings do not validate a biomarker in children, older adults or persistent severe adult TS.
