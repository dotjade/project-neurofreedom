## Bounded question completed

The [H18 mechanism and generalization audit](../../analyses/h18_mechanism/2026-11-07/report.md) separates evidence for whole behavioral treatment from evidence for a mechanism, individual component selection and everyday transfer. Historical global-urge analyses do not establish urge reduction as the mediator of benefit. A baseline-urge interaction concerns the whole CBIT package versus supportive therapy, not which component to select. Three secondary reports reuse the same original trials and are not independent replications.

A historical randomized reward/gameplay study supports trained suppression and a short observed-tic contrast, while broader severity contrasts did not demonstrate added benefit. Nonsignificance is not equivalence, and different significance labels do not test whether endpoint effects differ. Learning carryover, registration timing, blinding limits, uncertain endpoint denominators and incomplete harms information constrain interpretation. Positive habituation observations and symptom-substitution counterevidence are preserved. No validated personalized component rule, durable functional transfer or persistent-severe-adult benefit follows.
