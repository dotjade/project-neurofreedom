## What materially changed

### A synthetic counterexample now demonstrates the measurement boundary

The previously proposed paired-outcome exercise was actually executed October 19 after its plan/configuration were committed and read back. **SIMULATION ONLY:** 400 artificial units and 200 observation replicates shared identical tic changes across three constructed worlds with different pain changes. Tic-only indistinguishability is exact by construction; paired pain measurements distinguish some worlds, but cannot repair an entirely unobserved stratum. In the support-loss negative control, two worlds produce identical observed tic and pain data despite differing full-population pain means. Known-probability weighting recovers stipulated raw moments under supported observation models, not universally unbiased covariance or real-patient effects. This is methodological learning, not a treatment experiment, biological finding or sample-size recommendation. [Executed report](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/paired_outcomes/2026-10-19/report.md), [public pre-analysis commit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/commit/0a4677711ad6da4f7122a194cefa49b5ede3f097).

### Severe-adult physiology adds information, but not the missing clinical bridge

Lowor et al., PMID **40977255**, DOI **10.1002/mds.70043**, was published **22 September 2025**, not this week. Full-methods auditing confirmed six medically refractory adults aged 19–32, five contributing simultaneous CM–aGPi physiology. Tic-associated low-frequency power/coherence and phase-leading patterns constitute an additional state contrast, not a causal generator, pre-tic forecast or validated intervention rule. Onset-centered epochs contain post-onset information; repeated/overlapping epochs are not independent people. Statistical units and visit contributions remain incompletely specified. The exact five-pair signed-rank diagnostic (minimum two-sided doubled-tail p=.0625 under stated assumptions) shows why published small p values cannot be interpreted as that particular five-person calculation; it does not identify the authors' actual test or prove an error. Metric-specific nulls and the low-gamma Results/Discussion inconsistency are preserved. [Primary](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.70043), [audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/cm_agpi/2026-10-20/report.md).

Its trial **NCT05371041** has eight-enrollee results posted **25 September 2026**, not newly posted this week. A shared count of eight does not reconcile an impairment-inclusive 0–100 baseline with a 0–50 tic endpoint, or lead surgery, generator implantation and stimulation activation clocks. Planned responsive treatment is not proof of identical delivered exposure. No efficacy change or responder rate was reconstructed. Aggregate safety totals retain their own approximate two-year, non-systematic window; they are not device-attributable risks or comparable with the earlier smaller paper's safety statement. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05371041), [crosswalk](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/trial_crosswalk/2026-10-21/report.md).

Okun et al., PMID **38499664**, DOI **10.1038/s41598-024-57071-5**, published **18 March 2024**, tests different questions through short stimulation-state video, longitudinal recalled tic severity and MOS-36 function. Its favorable functional narrative coexists with multiplicity-adjusted nonsignificance and absent compatible aggregate functional uncertainty. Neither absence of benefit nor a responsive-over-continuous functional effect follows. Severe refractory adults remain explicitly represented, but exclusions of unstable psychiatric conditions limit transport. Cagle 2022 and Okun 2024 belong to **NCT02056873**, not independent trials; the mixed-trial Lowor preprint is not a third independent cohort. [Primary](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-57071-5), [estimand audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/okun_estimands/2026-10-22/report.md).

Official NCT02056873 history establishes changes from four original primary outcomes to one current endpoint, but does not establish selective reporting or motives. The 2019 protocol already contains 40% criteria, with internal motor/total and timing inconsistencies. Thus neither “unchanged from initial registration” nor “threshold invented in 2024” is justified. Masked state assessments can coexist with unmasked chronic treatment. [Official history](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02056873?tab=history), [dated audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/registration_history/2026-10-23/report.md).

### Newly indexed observational studies expand applicability questions, not treatment claims

Kaylor et al., PMID **42903303**, DOI **10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1948693**, published **October 6**, entered PubMed **October 22**. It directly documents persistent symptoms among selected adults aged 26–78. The 758 invitations, 334 responses, 292 with complete childhood/adult tic items and 258 retained cases are distinct stages. Current tics in 247/258 is not population persistence prevalence. Childhood-worst associations are retained, but recalled histories, lifetime OCD versus childhood wording, current medication versus recalled adult-worst outcome, unknown model-specific n and missing-data rules prevent prospective-risk or causal medication claims. ADHD/life-event severity nulls and printed impairment-OCD p=.050 are retained. Conditional denominator arithmetic is not a reconstructed model. [Primary](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1948693/full), [audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/adult_risk/2026-10-24/report.md).

Aldurayhim et al., PMID **42902960**, DOI **10.1002/mdc3.70882**, published **October 20**, entered PubMed **October 22**. In a 535-person first-visit cohort aged 3–72, 49 had ASD. Adjusted tic-attributed impairment was lower with ASD: −4.49 (95% CI −8.46 to −0.51), while adjusted total-tic severity was null-compatible: −0.31 (−2.91 to 2.29). The unadjusted impairment contrast was nonsignificant. These estimates answer different questions and may use different subsets. Model counts 483/485 do not identify adult-by-ASD representation. Age adjustment is not adult-stratified validation; lower measured tic impairment does not establish lower lived burden or prove reporting bias. Communication/informant differences are proposed explanations, not tested mechanisms. [Primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13683974/), [audit](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/analyses/asd_measurement/2026-10-25/report.md).

Two other new-to-repository papers supply negative or limiting information. Cif et al., PMID **42908833**, DOI **10.3389/fneur.2026.1904458**, published **October 7**, indexed **October 23**, had no consensus-positive tics in the external pediatric cohort. Overall multilabel accuracy cannot estimate tic sensitivity, adult Tourette performance or clinical benefit; selected calibration and reference-label-dependent results are preserved. Yu et al., PMID **42919181**, DOI **10.1186/s12888-026-08454-7**, lists **July 25** publication and **October 23** version-of-record/index dates. Its child/adolescent CHD–Tourette estimate is null-compatible, OR 1.48 (0.54–4.07), p=.45; subgroup table/prose disagreement prevents a directional severity conclusion. It was missed by the preceding bounded search, not newly published since that cutoff. [Cif](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2026.1904458), [Yu](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-026-08454-7), [October 24 scan](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/updates/2026-10-24/report.md), [October 25 scan](https://github.com/dotjade/project-neurofreedom/blob/main/updates/2026-10-25/report.md).

## Retire extrapolations, not untested biological hypotheses

Retire these operational inferences within the reviewed evidence: phase leading establishes causal initiation; post-onset detection establishes advance prediction; same-trial papers or mixed-trial preprints are independent replications; common n makes scores/clocks interchangeable; registry discrepancies establish researcher motive; recalled association validates prospective prognosis; age adjustment supplies adult subgroup validation; a lower tic-attributed scale proves lower overall burden; overall classification proves positive-tic sensitivity. No causal mechanism is disproved merely because its required inference bridge is undocumented. Retain actual associations and symptomatic findings; do not promote H16 responsive-DBS superiority, H17 mechanism-specific median-nerve stimulation, or H18 personalized CBIT matching, or claim they have been falsified by unrelated documentation checks. H07/H13 durable learning synergy and H19 resilience remain untested extensions, not discoveries.
