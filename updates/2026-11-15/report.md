## Historical bounded reanalysis: childhood tests and adult outcomes

Question and decision rule: if the broad functional prediction claim were supported by these published tables, expect an impairment association and at least one association robust to a 5% Holm sensitivity across the tabulated outcome families. This is a retrospective methodological screen, not a preregistered test of the underlying data.

[Barber et al., April 21, 2026, PMID 42012022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42012022/) ([publisher tables](https://acamh.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jcpp.70155)) followed 80 of 126 childhood-trial participants for about 11.7 years. Cognition was mainly assessed after treatment, with baseline substituted when post-treatment measurements were missing; the stop-signal reaction-time measure has 59 post-treatment, 8 baseline substitutions, and 13 missing. Treatment allocation was randomized, cognition was not. Models adjusted for original treatment, IQ and combined childhood ADHD/OCD; tic models also used the relevant post-treatment tic baseline. The selected follow-up cohort overlaps earlier reports; do not treat it as independent replication.

Methods: transcribe the rounded predictor p-values from Tables 3–5 in source row order. Count nominal p<.05, then calculate minimum Holm-adjusted and Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted values at the table and pooled levels. BH is a descriptive sensitivity here, not a guarantee under arbitrary dependent outcomes. Run python barber.py from this directory; all inputs are inside the script, no seed or participant data. Output: barber-results.json.

Results: 11 of 64 nominal associations (3/24 clinical, 3/24 function, 5/16 quality of life). None passes 5% Holm or BH either within each table or pooled. The smallest pooled Holm and BH values are .32 and .1813. Preserve nominal Stroop interference and adult tic severity (reported beta .34, p=.005), change accuracy and education (OR 1.08, p=.013), working memory and income (OR 1.11, p=.008), and quality-of-life associations. Table 3 impairment minimum p=.097; Table 4 employment minimum p=.052. This qualifies the abstract/discussion wording on impairment, consistent with its detailed results. Its remission endpoint is YGTSS total tic score below 14, not absence of tics. Identical printed triples for stop-signal reaction time and auditory consonant trigrams in Table 3 were retained as reported; no inference of data identity or unreported correction.

Uncertainty and next decision: the sensitivity depends on the exploratory family definition, rounded values, selected models and missingness. Non-survival does not prove no relationship. The study cannot establish a prediction instrument, cognitive training benefit, or a trajectory for people with persistent severe adult symptoms. Keep clinical severity, impairment, participation and quality of life separate; prioritize independent cohorts with prespecified outcomes, clear ascertainment and missingness, and out-of-sample calibration before using these measures as prognostic tools. No individual inference or treatment recommendation.
