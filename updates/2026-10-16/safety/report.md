## Limits and next verification

This is incremental public-notice surveillance, not exhaustive database enumeration. Returned-card counts include duplicates and are not unique-event counts. Combined searches do not identify which query produced each card. Date filters returned historical and unrelated material.

FDA safety-labeling records were not enumerated ingredient by ingredient, and labels were not compared byte by byte. National EU notices, private manufacturer communications and individual adverse-event records were not systematically searched. Indexing and retrieval delays can conceal recent notices. The two inaccessible Medline records remain a verification gap, not a positive safety finding.

The next scan should retain overlap, recheck FDA source freshness and the unresolved records, and deduplicate the two known watched records. No treatment recommendation, outreach, participant collection, transaction or repository commit was made. Only original summaries, metadata and links were saved.
